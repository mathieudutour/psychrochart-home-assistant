See how your room's air feels

A Home Assistant card that plots temperature and humidity together on a psychrometric chart, with a comfort zone that follows the seasons, the last day's trail and the dew point.

Add to Home Assistant with HACS View on GitHub

Hover the card for the dew point, axis labels and outdoor air. Double-click a reading for its sensor.

How to read it

Temperature runs from left to right and moisture, in grams of water per kilogram of air, from bottom to top. Every point is a state of the air.

  • Humidity curves

    Lines of constant relative humidity, every 10%. The top one is saturation: air at 100% can hold no more water.

  • Comfort zone

    Where most people feel comfortable. It shifts with the last 30 days of outdoor temperature, so it follows the seasons.

  • Trail and dot

    The last 24 hours, fading into the past, and the dot for right now, from blue when cool to red when warm.

  • Dew point

    Follow the moisture left until it saturates: any surface colder than that, like a window in winter, gets wet.

Install

Needs Home Assistant 2023.2 or newer.

With HACS

  1. In HACS, open the menu and choose Custom repositories.
  2. Add mathieudutour/psychrochart-home-assistant with the type Dashboard.
  3. Download Psychrometric Chart Card, then reload the browser.
  4. Edit a dashboard, Add card and pick Psychrometric Chart.
Open in HACS

Manually

  1. Download psychrochart-home-assistant.js from the latest release.
  2. Copy it to /config/www/.
  3. Add /local/psychrochart-home-assistant.js as a dashboard resource of type JavaScript module.
  4. Add the card from the card picker, or with the configuration above.
